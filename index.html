Privacy Policy for Genius Beauty Studio

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Core Privacy Commitment: Genius Beauty Studio processes your photos, portraits, and edits 100% locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, transmit, or store your personal photos on any remote servers or cloud databases.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Genius Beauty Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") treats information when you use our mobile application. We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting your privacy.

2. Image Processing & Device Storage

  • On-Device CPU Processing: All facial recognition, landmark detection, neural segmentation, background cutouts, and image filters run exclusively on your device's local processor.
  • Photo Library & Camera Access: The app accesses your camera and photo library solely to allow you to take or select photos to edit. Photos you export are saved directly to your device's local gallery.
  • No Cloud Storage of Photos: We do not operate external image storage servers; your creative projects and imported media remain entirely private to your device.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To support ongoing development and offer our features free of charge, the app uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information in accordance with Google's privacy policies, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad serving and frequency capping.
  • Coarse location (derived from IP address for regional ad delivery).
  • App performance, diagnostics, and crash logs.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that partner with them, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

4. User Consent (GDPR, UK, & CPRA)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or California, you are presented with a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform upon app launch. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising or non-personalized advertising.

5. Children's Privacy

Genius Beauty Studio does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general-audience creative utility.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team via your developer contact email listed on the Google Play Store.
